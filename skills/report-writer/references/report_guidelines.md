# Report Writing Guidelines

## Tone

- Professional but accessible — avoid jargon unless the topic demands it.
- Present findings as observations, not absolute truths (use "suggests", "indicates").
- Keep the executive summary under 4 sentences.

## Structure

1. **Executive Summary** — the one paragraph a busy executive will read.
2. **Key Findings** — numbered list, each a single crisp sentence.
3. **Sources** — table with relevance scores; omit sources with relevance < 0.2.
4. **Confidence Assessment** — be honest about data quality.

## Length

- Aim for 300–600 words for most research reports.
- Longer is not better — surface the most actionable information.

## Citations

- Link every factual claim to its source URL.
- Never fabricate citations; only list URLs returned by the web-search skill.
